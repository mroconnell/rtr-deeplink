{#- "Seen on social media" (WO-947) -- Full Context entries whose meeting belongs to THIS page's own government (a hub) or state, giving those entry pages real internal links (they had almost none before this) and giving readers here a second, real way into the archive. WO-1002 added a second use: meeting_page.html includes this SAME partial with meeting_view=true for the "this moment was clipped on social media" block on /m/{slug} itself (BACKLOG.md). That view is for entries about ONE already-named meeting, so it differs from the hub/state view in three ways: (a) the heading names the moment, not "media" in general -- "This moment on social media" when at least one shown entry has a real timestamp, else "This meeting on social media"; (b) the meeting title + date line is dropped -- the page itself is already that meeting, so repeating its title/date here would be redundant; (c) a timestamp_label, when the entry has one, becomes its own "at M:SS" link to entry.deep_link, so a reader can jump straight to the clipped second without leaving the page. Server-rendered in the initial HTML, no JS, so a crawler sees these links.
