Full jurisdiction detail table

Every column below is computed from real archived data, not a claim about what's theoretically supported — "Detail page" is the platform the meeting page itself lives on (e.g. Granicus, Swagit); "Video platform" is who actually serves the video, which sometimes differs (Legistar and CivicPlus pages usually hand video off to Granicus; PrimeGov and CivicWeb usually embed straight from YouTube — see "What about Platform XYZ?" on the main coverage page). "Instant transcript" means the source itself provided captions; "Audio transcript possible" means our own on-demand transcription could be attempted (not that it has been) — it's never possible for a YouTube-hosted video, since we can't pull audio out of a YouTube embed to transcribe. A jurisdiction with several archived meetings shows "yes" on any yes/no column if at least one of them has it, and its outcome/provider/example columns reflect its single best-covered meeting.
