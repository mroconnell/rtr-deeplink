1. Widen the narrow URL-path regex that decides which archived URLs count as meeting/video evidence with these tokens: civicmedia | vod | event | stream | live | show. 2. Watch the result-size effect: keep limit and collapse=urlkey, and record how many extra rows matchType=domain returns on a 10-government sample (table in the report).
