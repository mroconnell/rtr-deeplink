Red Tape Recordings

Local government puts out so many recordings, so many pages of minutes, that it's become transparent in name only — actually finding anything means sitting through hours of video. The government is so transparent, it's invisible. We built this so you don't have to.

Paste the link to any public meeting recording and get a transcript you can search and share — down to the exact moment. No account, no download, nothing to install.

This started because the founder, a housing advocate, spent hours every week watching city council and planning commission meetings for research, and got tired of doing it the slow way. Red Tape Recordings is for advocates, journalists, and organizers who need the same thing: to see through the bureaucracy again, quickly.

Questions, feedback, or a meeting that didn't work? ryan@how-to-adu.com
